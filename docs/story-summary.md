# City of Stars Teaser

The portal seems to be dormant today. No outlier activity. Resonance nominal. Keys clack as the tech takes detailed notes. “Day 143, 5:43 UTC. Project 19. Study 5. Reduction in anomaly appearance. World breach prediction T-minus 350 days, 14 hours, 37 minutes. 3 hours since the last registered spike. Security force on standby. 3 days, 12 hours since major incident. Log end”

A woman’s hand brushed over the shoulder of the technician. “Free after work Mr. Hotshot?”, the woman asks playfully. The technician hesitates to look away from the monitor and then finally spins his chair around to face the woman behind him, he looks up at her with sleep deprived eyes. She is exceptionally fair skinned for a tiefling. In the red light of the room she almost appeared golden. “What, too good for me after your big promotion?” Her tusks barely protruded from her smile. “Come on Sarah, you know you should not be here right now”, the technician dismisses her. “Oh come on, anomalies happen once a year. The last one was so small, security handled it almost instantly”. At that same moment, the terminal began to light up.

“WARNING, CLASS 5 ANOMALY DETECTED, EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY, EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY, WARNING CLASS 5 ANOMALY DETECTED, EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY, EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY”

The other technicians scrambled out the room, reaching to make it through the door as the auto lockdown procedure began to slowly seal the room. The department of magical affairs headquarters was lost almost as soon as the anomaly started. He is free.

## Story Summary

- The city of stars is a Dungeons and Dragons based campaign that takes place in Modern Day Los Angeles, California.
- The campaign features a hidden realm of magic and magical creatures in Los Angeles
- After being defeated by the humans in the great civil war, what remaining magical creatures were required to hide their existence to the humans or live in exile. The enforcement team known as Magitek, was formed to enforce the laws of magic and magical creatures.
- Magitek for many years now has been containing the more unpredictable forces of the world, and hiding the existence of magic from the public
- For about 100 years now they have been containing a strange energy like substance they discovered that seemed to cause many people to vanish across the country. They've simply named it "the Void"
- every day, Magitek control officers check on the containment status of "the Void". As it morphed slowly from a spec of energy into a humanoid form, it is assumed he is some manifestation from a dark god.
- one day, the void was able to breach containment and absorb the souls of the Magitek officers who fought against him into his being

- after escaping, the void attempts to absorb the souls of the last remaining sorcerers with the ability to manipulate the weave
- the void is able to form attachments with these sorcerers, but is blocked by the weave until the will of the sorcerer's breaks
- once he absorbs the sorcerers, the void will be able to fully manipulate reality into a world of his own choosing, ending life as we know it
