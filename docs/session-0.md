# City of Stars Session 0 Narrative Intro

The camera pans over the Los Angeles cityscape. It darts over the US bank building and begins to drop, lower and lower. The camera then sort of pauses for a moment, floating there looking into the empty space between two large buildings. The one on the left is an old, dirt stained, brick building. Windows stay open in the summer heat and dilapidated AC units dangle precariously off the building. There is a sort of fog to the air, more than the usual smog. The camera vibrates for a moment, as if a surge of wind caused it to bob. It zooms in closer on a point in the space in front of it. A J.J Abrams style lens flare momentarily fills the frame as the point in space grows a singular dark dot. The dot grows, its shape spiking like an urchin, but yet very fluid and rhythmic as it expands. Once about the size of a baseball, the dot spins for a moment, and almost instantaneously jolts into an open window in the brick building to the left of the screen. The camera spins to catch up to it and shakily zooms to it flying towards a man asleep on the couch. The camera begins to zoom and captures the dot expanding on the man's face with a parasitic nature quite like a “face-hugger”. The man gasps awake, and then sits in his seat frozen for about a minute as the dot phases into him, appearing to become part of him. His head then violently pulls back as he screams to the ceiling, his body convulsing. His body goes limp for a moment, and the room goes quiet. He is snoring. The man then proceeds to wake up again, this time unfazed and unaware of his capture.

### Magitek Encounter

- Each player in the party will control a Magitek officer attempting to contain the void in his containment facility
- The void will escape but the players will get to perform legendary moves on behalf of the Magitek officers
- the Magitek officers are similar in style to storm troopers, but with a magical inclination in their weapon choice and use of spells

### The actions the character may perform are highlighted below, as well as their main objective in this section

#### Main Objectives

- Get dressed
- Leave the apartment

#### Actions

- Retrieve necktie from ceiling: (The player must make an acrobatics check to attempt to balance on a stool, or may do a perception check for another option)
- Find your other shoe (The player must make a perception check when looking out the window to see their shoe is on the street below)
- Find your vape (The player can do investigation to replay the scenario of where they put their vape, DM discretion on perception for finding it)
- Clean up (This is mainly for flavor, but you may give the player inspiration if desired)
- Take out garbage (Must make a strength check to throw the bag, The player may find up to 5 dollars that they left with a receipt they threw away by mistake)
- Look at your phone (Mainly flavor, given the interactions, this could give a future disadvantage or advantage on a roll of the DMs choosing)

#### Once the player attempts to grab their shoe

- You see your shoe in the street, you’re so close to having two shoes! then everything will be all right!

- Ask the player for a perception check, if they achieve a 10 or higher, they notice that a street sweeper is coming down the street and is going to hit the player’s shoe.

- If they notice, they may attempt to run and get the shoe before the street sweeper hits it, DM discretion on if they make it or get hit by the street sweeper (or it stops before hitting them).

- Recommended random D20 roll: 8 or lower, the street sweeper doesn’t stop

- If the shoe is hit by the street sweeper, it can either be ruined, or be flung into the alleyway next to the building.

- Once the player gets their shoe, they will see a strange character in the alleyway:

- You see what appears to be a goblin in the alleyway across from you. He has pale green skin and stands much smaller than a full-grown man. You can’t believe your eyes, and wonder if you really drank that much. Goblins aren’t real. They can’t be. But, he appears to be looking through a bag of items, and is very much real a goblin

- Ask the player their plan of action, do they seek to ignore the goblin or go in the alleyway? If the player attempted to go into the alley to receive their shoe, flung by the streetsweeper it is too late. If the player traverses into the alleyway they will be attacked by 3 other goblins. This is a scripted scenario

- As you step into the alleyway, the goblin’s head sharply turns to you, pointing and screams “He sees us! Get his ass!”. You are violently grabbed by several small green hands and pulled into an even smaller outlet of the alleyway. A blur of green fists and feet pummel you and you can feel your face beginning to swell as your eyes go black, your mind begins to fill with all the dark feelings deep inside you. Fuck my life, I deserve this, i’m such a piece of filth, no one would care if I die here … good riddance. In that moment, you feel a sudden surge of energy, it feeds from your hate, your self loathing, it draws from you. All that darkness inside you turns to bloodlust. You are free! The ground begins to ooze with a pool of black tar-like fluid. While you are visionless at the moment, you feel alive! Nutrients, you need more! More! To be full again! The mandrels growing for your tar covered body have drained the goblins dry. Nothing is left. Then suddenly you feel a tear, a horrible tear. Part of you is leaving. You rip at your skull because your brain is on fire. The ooze peels back with your fingers and you feel the rest of it almost jump off you. The ooze centers into a large dot once again, floating there for a moment while you remain stunned. The ooze then pulses and forms into a man. It’s you but, perfect, as if built from greek marble and stained completely black. The man laughs and claps his hands together as if starting a film, “time for the show” he says and then poofs into nothingness. Your eyes begin to darken again as you hear someone running up behind you. “Call an ambulance! He looks like he jumped!” You try to stay awake, but you are just not strong enough.
